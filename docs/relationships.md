# Relationships in this fork

This fork of [Mundi](https://github.com/tjalis/Mundi) reworks how relationships feel to play. People in your world
have feelings of their own: for you, and for each other. Those feelings grow with what really happens in your scenes,
fade when you drift apart, and lead people to act on their own. This page describes it from a player's point of view.

## Feelings that go both ways

- **Everyone has their own feelings.** How Kaho feels about Saya isn't how Saya feels about Kaho. One can adore the
  other while the other can't stand them, and the AI is told when that's the case.
- **People can dislike you.** Friendship now goes below zero, through *Wary*, *Dislikes* and *Hates*. Someone who
  dislikes you turns down plans and invitations, won't let you in, and doesn't invite you. Someone who hates you
  doesn't want you around at all.
- **Love can outweigh dislike.** Someone with a crush on you may still say yes to a date, even while they resent
  you. Enemies to lovers is possible, and the card says "Love-hate".
- **Feelings in words.** Cards show what both bars mean together: *Soulmates*, *In love*, *Infatuated*, *Love-hate*,
  *Platonic*. The AI hears the same words.
- **Playing as someone else ("Play as").** While you play a character, their own feelings wait. When you switch
  back, they can either take on what everyone came to feel about them, or carry on from where they were (your
  choice).

## Friendship and romance are separate

- **Friendship never turns into romance by itself.** A best friend can stay just a best friend for years, and a
  stranger at a bar can be flirting with you on the first night.
- **Romance grows from romantic moments**: flirting, dates, intimacy. Talking alone with someone matters: their warm
  or flirty replies raise romance one-on-one, but not in a crowd.
- **Romance fades without contact.** A fling cools off within a couple of weeks if you never see each other again. A
  partner you ignore for a long time slowly falls out of love.
- **Partners can leave you.** A partner who no longer loves you, or has come to dislike you, ends it after a few days.
  They tell you themselves, and they warn you first (see below).
- **Something casual.** "Keep it casual?" starts a fling, or friends with benefits once you're friends. It isn't
  being a couple: no moving in, no baby, and other casual partners aren't jealous.
- **Staying the night is always something you can ask.** Close friends can stay over as friends. A night together
  depends on how that person is about intimacy (see [Who people are](#who-people-are)). Their answer comes from the
  scene, not a fixed rule.
- **Commitment still needs friendship.** Dating, getting engaged and getting married need both romance and real
  friendship.

## Scenes that count

- **Every scene is judged when it ends.** When you leave a place, sleep, skip time, or someone you talked with
  leaves, the AI reads the scene and says how it changed what each person feels about you. A kind gesture, a
  flirt, a fight or an insult all count, and the change is applied right away instead of the next morning.
- **Changes follow your messages.** Swipe a reply five times and keep the fifth: only the fifth counts. The same goes
  for regenerating, editing or deleting messages, until the scene is over.
- **Long conversations count more, without running away.** A long warm talk raises friendship a lot more than a quick
  hello. Each gain counts a little less as it piles up, and the pace comes back over the next hours. Nobody goes from
  stranger to good friend in one evening, but nothing ever stops completely either.
- **"Needs some time."** When someone has warmed up to you very fast, their card says they need some time, and the
  story reflects it.
- **No more lost friendship from misread words.** Mentioning the gym or calling a movie stupid no longer costs
  anything, and neither does a character's sad reply. Losses come from what actually happened in the scene.
- **How the day went.** After sleeping, you see every change of the day for each person, with the reasons.

## Who people are

Each person, you included, can have:

- **A gender.** Every world has its own list (Woman, Man and Non-binary to start; the world creator can change it).
  The built-in characters already have one. The AI is told everyone's gender and pronouns in the scene.
- **Attraction.** Who they can fall for, per gender: "Everyone", "Only women", "No one", or exact amounts per gender.
  Someone not attracted to your gender never falls for you and always turns you down. Someone only a little attracted
  falls for you more slowly. The dating app only shows people who could be attracted to you.
- **How they are about intimacy.** *Free spirit* (a spark is enough), *Passionate*, *Steady* (the usual), *Slow to
  trust*, *Devoted*, or custom; plus "Only with a partner" for people who don't do anything casual. It decides when
  they're ready for something casual or a night together, and the AI plays them accordingly.
- **Exclusive or open.** An exclusive partner is hurt when you see someone else. An open one isn't.
- **How fast their feelings grow**, for friendship and romance: slow, normal or fast.

You set all of this in one panel, in the in-game People tab, in the new game (for your cast and for yourself), in the
World creator for a world's own characters, and in your saved cast presets. Two helpers:

- **Suggest from the cards.** Your AI reads each character card once and proposes a gender, attraction and intimacy
  style. Nothing changes until you apply the suggestions.
- **Save to card.** Keeps a person's settings on their character card, so every new game with them starts from
  there.

## People take the initiative

Characters don't wait for you to drive every relationship.

- **Moments.** The first time someone's feelings for you reach a new level, the next scene with them shows it: they
  ask you something personal, open up about their past, tell you what you mean to them, show small signs of
  attraction, hint at a crush or confess it, or say they love you. If their feelings sour, they tell you plainly what
  bothers them.
- **They ask you.** Someone who would say yes if you asked may ask you instead: to be a couple, to keep it casual, to
  marry, about having a baby, or to stay the night. Answer in your own words; the game understands your reply and it
  counts exactly as if you had asked.
- **They text you.** Someone with a crush may text you a date. A casual partner may ask you over tonight. Accept,
  decline, or say "not yet".
- **Warning signs.** A partner you haven't seen in days texts that they miss you. If things keep getting worse, they
  write that you need to talk, and bring it up when you meet, before they end it. A good scene together turns it
  around.
- **Friends check in** when you haven't seen them for a week.
- **Grudges.** Insult someone, stand them up, or cancel your plans, and they're hurt for some days: they bring it up,
  turn down your plans and don't invite you. Apologize sincerely in a scene and it's over (with a little bonus);
  otherwise it fades on its own.
- **Jealousy.** A partner who wants to be your only one and sees you flirt with someone else confronts you, right
  there. If they hear a rumor about your date with someone else, they text you about it. Two people who both have a
  crush on you become rivals.
- **What they want.** The AI knows what each person in the scene wants from you right now (an apology, to be asked
  out, something casual, to get closer, some space) and plays it.
- **Opinions spread.** Someone who adores you, or can't stand you, talks about you to their close friends, and slowly
  colors what those friends think of you.
- **Not too much.** At the default setting, people start at most one thing on their own a day, and each person only
  every few days. You can make it rarer, more frequent, or turn it off.

## Couples and feelings among the world's people

- **The world's people get to know each other.** Coworkers, neighbors and housemates grow closer from time spent
  together. Rivals drift apart.
- **Couples form from real feelings,** when two people fall for each other, not at random. They get engaged and
  married when both love and like each other, and break up when the feelings fade. Two people can also keep things
  casual, which turns into dating or quietly ends.
- **Attraction, family and age are respected.** Nobody pairs up against their attraction, family members never do,
  and minors are never part of any romance.
- **You hear about it.** New couples, breakups and one-sided crushes reach you as news when you know the people.
- In your starting relations (new game, World creator, cast presets), each person can feel differently about the
  other: Kaho can see Saya as a close friend while Saya can't stand her.

## Where to change things

- **World → Rules → Relationships** (the globe button in a game): judging scenes at their end, how often characters
  take the initiative (off, rarely, normal, often), whether opinions spread through friends, open relationships, what
  happens to feelings with "Play as", and, under *Advanced*, how fast feelings grow and fade.
- **World → People**: each person's gender, attraction, intimacy, relationship style and pace; feelings between any
  two people; "Suggest from the cards" and "Save to card".
- **Show the numbers.** For the curious, a debug view (World → Rules → Relationships, Advanced) shows the exact values,
  how fast each relationship is growing right now, what each message changed (with undo), and what people are about
  to bring up.
- **AI context**: the new blocks ("Gender and attraction", "Their initiatives") can be switched off like the others.

A note on AI usage: judging a scene is one short extra AI request per scene (none for scenes with only a few lines),
and the game also asks a short yes/no question after someone asks you something. "Suggest from the cards" uses one
request per card, only when you click it.

## Your existing games

Saves from the original Mundi open in this fork. They're upgraded once, automatically, with a backup kept in the save.
Everyone keeps their friendship and romance with you, couples stay together, and the world's people start their
feelings for each other from their existing ties. Nobody has a gender or special attraction until you set them, so
nothing changes behind your back.
