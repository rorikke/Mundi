# Mundi roadmap

Ideas planned for later. Nothing here is promised for a date.

## Scenarios

- **High school:** a scenario inspired by school slice-of-life visual novels: class periods, clubs, the rooftop at
  lunch, festivals and exams on a school calendar, classmates and teachers with their own routines.

## Business

- **Tycoon:** stakes in a company (a seed, a large one, a majority), its value as the score: the projects you back, a
  funding round, a listing, selling out; Rina's company, Nanami's band, Lisa's studio, a club with Sayuri. Also the end
  game's next income: the museum still takes 5–6 game years on ¥1,000,000 a week, and never completes on ¥500,000.
- **The other worlds' businesses:** Porto Alto's, Nova Vega's and Valdoria's small businesses on the business engine,
  with upgrades, tiers and stories.

## Homes and shops

- **Homes:** parties at the second home with guests staying the night; homes for sale in the other worlds.
- **Shops:** the other worlds' shops grown the way Tokyo's was (Porto Alto's, the Shared House's).

## Storylines

- Storylines for the other worlds' people, and a second storyline for a person once their first has ended.
- Tuning from play: how long the first stage and the wait between last chances should be, and how many last chances a
  story gets.

## Rumors

- **Habits people notice:** what you do often, seen by the same people, becomes a rumor in the world's words ("{{user}}
  is at the pachinko parlor almost every night") instead of a trait. It counts what habits used to count (actions,
  place tags or hours, so many times in so many days) and remembers who was there each time; those who saw it most
  start it, and it spreads, garbles and dies out like any rumor, so only people the gossip reached know it (a partner
  can hear about your nights in Kabukichō; nobody else simply knows). Keep it up and it starts again once the old one
  has died out. No trait, bonus or app panel; Quiet a rumor stops it. Only habits worth gossip (not showering or cooking
  at home); a world's habit without its own words gets a plain one. Runs with the rumors system. Habits as they were
  (removed in schema 9) are in git history: `git log -S HabitDef`.

## Worlds

- **Medieval:** paused; it gets the systems built for the modern worlds once they are done.
- **World creator, "From lorebooks":** your AI reads lorebooks you choose and builds a world from them.
- **The World creator from inside a game:** everything the creator can change, from ☰ → This world while you play
  (plan and limitations: [`world-edit-in-game.md`](world-edit-in-game.md)).
- **Tokyo in Portuguese and Spanish:** the world's own text (places, things to do, stories) in
  `worlds/tokyo/lang/pt.json` and `es.json`, as Porto Alto has.
