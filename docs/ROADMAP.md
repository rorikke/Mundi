# Mundi roadmap

Ideas planned for later. Nothing here is promised for a date.

## Scenario mode

- **High school.** A scenario inspired by school slice-of-life visual novels (anime): class periods, clubs,
  the rooftop at lunch, festivals and exams on a school calendar, classmates and teachers with their own
  routines. Like every scenario: Life-Sim only, one place as the stage, life outside happens off-screen.

## Engine

- **Running a business: the next track.** Three tracks are in Tokyo now (a café of your own, the executive's floor,
  and hotels: Nest Capsule, Ryokan Kasumi-sō and Hotel Coco Palace; see
  [Running a business](improvements.md#running-a-business)). Next, on the same engine:
  - **Tycoon:** buying a stake in a company (a seed stake, a large one, a majority), its value as the score: the
    projects you back, a funding round, a listing, selling out; Rina's company, Nanami's band, Lisa's studio, a club
    with Sayuri.
  - Life goals for the café, the hotels and the executive's floor are in (A café to remember, Hotelier, The corner
    office: see [Life goals and life points](improvements.md#life-goals-and-life-points)); perhaps the other worlds'
    fixed small businesses (`property`) moved
    onto the same engine.

- **End-game spending: homes and a collection.** In Tokyo now: the shop ([Shopping](improvements.md#shopping)), homes
  you own with their own pictures, rooms and parties ([Your own home](improvements.md#your-own-home)), letting them
  ([Letting a home you own](improvements.md#letting-a-home-you-own): a slow, steady income, an Azabu residence's
  price back in about seven game years), the record of what you've lived ([Tokyo memories](improvements.md#tokyo-memories)),
  a museum of your own with Tamaki, its royalties, the rival collector Ayame Mikage and the dealer Kaede Utsumi with the
  Mikage Foundation's loans and cases ([A museum of your own](improvements.md#a-museum-of-your-own-and-tamaki)), its
  Nature and Before Edo halls ([Nature and Before Edo](improvements.md#nature-and-before-edo)), and the end game's life
  goals ([Life goals and life points](improvements.md#life-goals-and-life-points)). Next:
  - **Homes, later:** parties at the second home with guests staying the night, and homes for sale in the other worlds.
  - **The copyist** (a short story for later): fakes turn up in Kaede's van, and Tamaki, Ayame and Kaede trace them
    to the hand that painted the Mikage "Sesshū". A story with choices for all three and an adult side when adult
    content is on; nothing to buy twice, and no fake ever stands in a real piece's way.
  - **TODO, the museum's pace:** in the long simulated runs, a player putting the museum first completes its base halls
    5.0–5.9 game years after taking on the kura on ¥1,000,000 a week (3.2–4.2 on ¥1,500,000), with the Mikage
    Foundation's loans and cases and the museum's royalties; without them it took 6.7–8.0. On ¥500,000 it still ends
    at 97–99% after eight years. With the end game's life goals followed and their rewards bought (Natural history's
    finds, Every hall's royalties, Patron of the arts' renown, a press day, the dealer's tip, the workshop's priority) it's
    5.1–5.9 on ¥1,000,000 and 2.9–4.1 on ¥1,500,000; letting three Azabu homes bought from the museum's money makes it
    5.6–6.3 and 3.6–5.0 (the rent doesn't make their price back before the museum is complete). Prices stay as they are;
    the museum's money should grow instead: next, stakes in companies (Tycoon, above).
  - The other worlds' shops grown the same way (Porto Alto's, the Shared House's).

- **Storylines: more of them.** Tokyo has 43, for 32 of its people (13 of them adult; see
  [Storylines](improvements.md#storylines)), and Tokyo memories keeps how each one you saw ended. Next:
  - Storylines for the other worlds' people (the World creator can already write them), and a second storyline for a
    person once their first has ended.
  - Tuning from play: how long the first stage and the wait between last chances should be, and how many last chances
    a story gets.

## Worlds

- **Medieval:** paused; it gets the systems built for the modern worlds once they are done.
- **World creator, "From lorebooks":** your AI reads lorebooks you choose and builds a world from them.
- **The world creator from inside a game:** everything the creator can change, changeable from ☰ → This world while
  you play; a game in a built-in world gets its own copy of the world first (plan and limitations:
  [`world-edit-in-game.md`](world-edit-in-game.md)).
- **Tokyo: a photographer's job.** Photography is a skill now (it trains at scenic places and with cameras, and counts
  in a few existing jobs; see [Shopping](improvements.md#photography)). Next, a job of its own: a studio to work from (a
  new place and its picture), assignments for magazines, weddings and the city's festivals and events, and an adult side
  when adult content is on.
- **Tokyo in Portuguese and Spanish:** the world's own text (places, things to do, stories) in
  `worlds/tokyo/lang/pt.json` and `es.json`, as Porto Alto has; the interface is already translated.
