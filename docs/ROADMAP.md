# Mundi roadmap

Ideas planned for later. Nothing here is promised for a date.

## Scenario mode

- **High school.** A scenario inspired by school slice-of-life visual novels (anime): class periods, clubs,
  the rooftop at lunch, festivals and exams on a school calendar, classmates and teachers with their own
  routines. Like every scenario: Life-Sim only, one place as the stage, life outside happens off-screen.

## Engine

- **Running a business: the next tracks.** The first two tracks are in Tokyo now (a café of your own and the
  executive's floor; see [Running a business](improvements.md#running-a-business)). Next, on the same engine:
  - **Hospitality:** a capsule hotel (Nest Capsule, bought out from its manager's rank or bought), the ryokan in
    Hakone as a partnership through Shizuka's story, and a love hotel with adult content on (Hotel Coco Palace, with
    Kaoru's window and notebook: the guests you know as stories, and discretion as a choice). Rooms upgraded one at a
    time, occupancy by hour, weekday, season and festival, and people renting a room for a business of their own.
  - **Tycoon:** buying a stake in a company (a seed stake, a large one, a majority), its value as the score: the
    projects you back, a funding round, a listing, selling out; Rina's company, Nanami's band, Lisa's studio, a club
    with Sayuri.
  - Life goals and keepsakes for each track, and perhaps the other worlds' fixed small businesses (`property`) moved
    onto the same engine.

## Worlds

- **Medieval:** paused; it gets the systems built for the modern worlds once they are done.
- **World creator, "From lorebooks":** your AI reads lorebooks you choose and builds a world from them.
- **The world creator from inside a game:** everything the creator can change, changeable from ☰ → This world while
  you play; a game in a built-in world gets its own copy of the world first (plan and limitations:
  [`world-edit-in-game.md`](world-edit-in-game.md)).
- **Tokyo in Portuguese and Spanish:** the world's own text (places, things to do, stories) in
  `worlds/tokyo/lang/pt.json` and `es.json`, as Porto Alto has; the interface is already translated.
